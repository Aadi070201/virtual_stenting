### SR-41: Foreshortening-free lengths

With two views of a vessel (DICOM geometry, the same two landmarks in each), the vessel is reconstructed in 3D and its lumen profile, lengths and FFR follow the 3D centreline. Motion of the heart between the runs is estimated and removed (across the epipolar lines). The reconstruction is checked: the views' separation, the mean gap between paired rays and the reprojection residual give a warning when the geometry does not fit, and the figure shows the 3D centreline projected back onto both views. A reliability check refits the curve with smoother settings and warns when the length depends on them or the 3D path departs from a traced path. Stent plans from a single view state that their lengths are foreshortened.

- **Code:** `src/vstent/imaging/twoview.py::reconstruct`, `src/vstent/imaging/twoview.py::estimate_shift`, `src/vstent/imaging/twoview.py::Reconstruction.warnings`, `src/vstent/imaging/twoview.py::Reconstruction.reliable`, `src/vstent/inference/pipeline.py::analyse_two_views`, `src/vstent/inference/figures.py::plot_two_view_analysis`, `src/vstent/cli.py::SINGLE_VIEW_LENGTHS`

## Physics

### SR-14: Reduced-order FFR model

FFR is computed from ΔP(Q) = a·Q + b·Q² (Poiseuille viscous loss and the expansion loss Kt·ρ/2·(1/A_min − 1/A_max)², Kt = 1.52) with the closed-form flow root; FFR starts at 1 and never increases along the vessel; the M1 sanity bands hold.

- **Code:** `src/vstent/physics/solver.py::solve_ffr`, `src/vstent/physics/solver.py::viscous_coefficient`, `src/vstent/physics/solver.py::expansion_coefficient`
- **Verified by** tests: [`test_uniform_tube_poiseuille_fixed_flow`](../../tests/test_physics.py), [`test_sudden_expansion_matches_borda_carnot`](../../tests/test_physics.py), [`test_ffr_starts_at_one_and_never_increases`](../../tests/test_physics.py), [`test_sanity_bands_3mm_60mm_10mm_cosine`](../../tests/test_physics.py), [`test_distal_ffr_strictly_decreases_with_stenosis`](../../tests/test_physics.py), [`test_grid_convergence`](../../tests/test_physics.py)
- **Verified by** reports: [physics_checks.md](../../reports/physics_checks.md)
- **Risk control for:** H-06, H-14
- **Status:** verified
