# Hazard analysis (ISO 14971 draft)

> **Research use only — not for clinical decisions.** Draft regulatory groundwork for review by a qualified person; not regulatory advice.

Generated by `vstent regulatory` from `docs/regulatory/hazards.yaml`. Do not edit by hand.

Method and scope: [RISK_MANAGEMENT_PLAN.md](RISK_MANAGEMENT_PLAN.md). Risks are evaluated for the intended clinical use ([INTENDED_USE.md](INTENDED_USE.md)), not for today's research use, in which no patient decision is made. Probabilities marked *measured* come from generated reports; *estimated* ones are engineering judgement to be replaced by data.

**20 hazards.** Before the risk controls: 16 unacceptable, 3 reduce, 1 acceptable. After: 15 unacceptable, 1 reduce, 4 acceptable.

**15 residual risks are unacceptable for clinical use.** This is why the software is restricted to research use (SR-01). The *needed* line of each hazard is the evidence or control that would lower it.

## Risk policy

One use = one analysed vessel.

| Severity | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Negligible | inconvenience or temporary discomfort |
| 2 | Minor | temporary injury or impairment not needing medical intervention |
| 3 | Serious | injury needing medical intervention, e.g. a repeat revascularisation |
| 4 | Critical | life-threatening injury or permanent impairment, e.g. myocardial infarction |
| 5 | Catastrophic | death |

| Probability | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Improbable | fewer than 1 in 100,000 uses |
| 2 | Remote | 1 in 100,000 to 1 in 10,000 uses |
| 3 | Occasional | 1 in 10,000 to 1 in 1,000 uses |
| 4 | Probable | 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 100 uses |
| 5 | Frequent | 1 in 100 uses or more |

Risk index = severity × probability: ≤ 4 acceptable; 5–9 reduce as far as possible and weigh against the benefit; ≥ 10 unacceptable.

| Severity \ probability | P1 Improbable | P2 Remote | P3 Occasional | P4 Probable | P5 Frequent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S5 Catastrophic | 5 R | 10 U | 15 U | 20 U | 25 U |
| S4 Critical | 4 A | 8 R | 12 U | 16 U | 20 U |
| S3 Serious | 3 A | 6 R | 9 R | 12 U | 15 U |
| S2 Minor | 2 A | 4 A | 6 R | 8 R | 10 U |
| S1 Negligible | 1 A | 2 A | 3 A | 4 A | 5 R |

A: acceptable, R: reduce, U: unacceptable.

## Hazards

### H-01: Stenosis severity underestimated

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The lumen edges are blurred in the angiogram, the segmentation fills a tight lesion, or the lesion is narrowed along the X-ray; QCA reads a smaller %DS than the true one.
- **Hazardous situation:** The FFR shown is too high (above 0.80) for a haemodynamically significant lesion.
- **Harm:** Revascularisation deferred for an ischaemic lesion: angina, myocardial infarction, urgent revascularisation.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Measured. CADICA test patients, intensity edges (the product default) with the D53 segmenter: weighted kappa 0.13 against expert grades (0.14 with M5), median measured %DS 44% for lesions graded 70–90%. Rendered vessels: edges read 95% lesions as 37–59% DS.
- **Controls:** SR-10 Lumen edges from the image intensity; SR-11 QCA accuracy on rendered vessels; SR-12 Segmentation gaps and unmeasurable lumens; SR-23 Input interval (Monte Carlo); SR-24 Grey-zone flag; SR-02 Plain-language warnings with every result; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Measured with the controls in place (the CADICA result is the product's own method). Intervals and warnings inform the user but do not correct the compression.
- **Evidence:** [stenosis_grading.md](../../reports/stenosis_grading.md), [lumen_synthetic.md](../../reports/lumen_synthetic.md)
- **Needed:** Lesion-accurate QCA on real angiograms (agreement with expert or core-lab QCA), then agreement with invasive FFR (ICADD or a clinical study).

### H-02: Stenosis severity overestimated

- **Cause / sequence of events:** Overlapping or crossing vessels, poor filling, a foreshortened bend or the catheter in the path create a false narrowing.
- **Hazardous situation:** The FFR shown is too low (0.80 or below) for a lesion that is not significant.
- **Harm:** Unnecessary PCI: dissection, perforation, peri-procedural myocardial infarction, bleeding on antiplatelet therapy, later stent thrombosis or restenosis.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P4 Probable = 16 unacceptable. Estimated. On CADICA no lesion graded 0–50% was read as ≥ 90% DS (0 of 219 measured with the D53 segmenter; 0 of 223 with M5); smaller overestimates were not quantified, and nothing is measured against FFR.
- **Controls:** SR-10 Lumen edges from the image intensity; SR-06 End-diastolic analysis frame in the opacified phase; SR-13 Analysed path shown on the image; SR-24 Grey-zone flag; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P4 Probable = 16 unacceptable. Not measured after the controls; unchanged.
- **Evidence:** [stenosis_grading.md](../../reports/stenosis_grading.md)
- **Needed:** Agreement with invasive FFR; false-positive rate in vessels with FFR > 0.80.

### H-03: Wrong pixel size

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The image has no calibrated pixel size at the vessel (exported frames, or DICOM pixel spacing at the detector without magnification correction); the default is assumed.
- **Hazardous situation:** Absolute diameters and lengths are wrong by the scale error, so the FFR is wrong in either direction and the stent diameter is mis-sized.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02; an oversized stent can dissect or perforate, an undersized one can be malapposed (thrombosis, restenosis).
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Estimated from the data seen so far: the public angiograms (ARCADE, CADICA) carry no pixel size. Before P5 the loader offered the DICOM detector spacing, which is magnified by SID / SOD: on synthetic cases that overstated lengths by 20% (median) and changed FFR by 0.023 (median; up to 0.16) from geometry alone.
- **Controls:** SR-40 Pixel size from the projection geometry; SR-41 Foreshortening-free lengths; SR-09 Pixel size and units; SR-02 Plain-language warnings with every result; SR-23 Input interval (Monte Carlo); SR-26 Generic catalogue and sizing rule
- **After controls:** P3 Occasional = 12 unacceptable. Measured on synthetic cases: DICOM input is corrected to the isocentre (4% median error at the vessel in one view, 0.008 median FFR error from geometry alone); two views use the pixel size at each 3D point. Exported frames without DICOM keep the assumed default with its warning and ±10% interval. Not yet verified on real C-arm data.
- **Evidence:** [two_view_synthetic.md](../../reports/two_view_synthetic.md)
- **Needed:** The geometry confirmed on real two-view DICOM or a phantom of known size; catheter calibration for frames without DICOM.

### H-04: Total occlusion read as open

- **Cause / sequence of events:** In a single frame the segmentation bridges an occluded segment (collateral filling, overlapping vessels), and the QCA measures a lumen there.
- **Hazardous situation:** An FFR is shown for an occluded vessel supplied by collaterals, which the flow model does not represent.
- **Harm:** Wrong treatment decision for a chronic total occlusion; delayed treatment.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Measured on CADICA: 9 of 11 total occlusions were measured with the D53 segmenter (10 with M5) and none was read as ≥ 90% DS, so the occlusion warning never fired on a real occlusion.
- **Controls:** SR-12 Segmentation gaps and unmeasurable lumens; SR-02 Plain-language warnings with every result; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. The occlusion warning works on rendered vessels only.
- **Evidence:** [stenosis_grading.md](../../reports/stenosis_grading.md)
- **Needed:** Occlusion detection that works on real runs (e.g. delayed or retrograde filling over the cine) and an explicit user confirmation that the vessel is open.

### H-05: Lesion length underestimated

- **Cause / sequence of events:** One projection foreshortens the vessel or overlaps it with another; the measured lesion is shorter than the real one.
- **Hazardous situation:** The planned stent is too short to cover the lesion.
- **Harm:** Geographic miss: restenosis, repeat revascularisation.
- **Before controls:** S3 Serious × P5 Frequent = 15 unacceptable. Measured on synthetic cases: one view read the vessel 13% short (median; 16% of views within 5% of the true length).
- **Controls:** SR-41 Foreshortening-free lengths; SR-13 Analysed path shown on the image; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P4 Probable = 12 unacceptable. Two views remove foreshortening on synthetic cases (0.6% median length error, 95% of cases within 5%), but only when the user supplies a second view; single-view plans state that their lengths are foreshortened. Not yet verified on real data.
- **Evidence:** [two_view_synthetic.md](../../reports/two_view_synthetic.md)
- **Needed:** Two-view lengths verified on real data (a phantom or IVUS pullback length), and two views in the app.

### H-06: Reduced-order physics error

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The model assumes steady, fully developed flow, an area-only expansion loss, a fixed hyperaemic velocity and a healthy microcirculation; real lesions, diffuse disease, microvascular dysfunction and collaterals differ.
- **Hazardous situation:** The FFR shown is systematically wrong for the patient.
- **Harm:** As H-01 (FFR too high) or H-02 (too low).
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Measured where possible. FDA benchmark nozzle, abrupt exit, Re 500: modelled loss 0.61 × the measured median (FFR too high). Synthetic stand-in study with the product's fixed assumptions, scored against its own physics: limits of agreement −0.135 to +0.152, accuracy at 0.80 of 0.902. Real physiology (DEFINE-FLOW, 455 lesions): the stenosis-free hyperemic velocity is 0.21–0.33 m/s (median, by the Doppler mean-to-peak factor) against the fixed 0.35 m/s, and P1's velocity range holds only 27–56% of lesions. Nothing is measured against invasive FFR from images.
- **Controls:** SR-14 Reduced-order FFR model; SR-15 Microvascular resistance calibration; SR-16 Vessel-tree physics; SR-23 Input interval (Monte Carlo); SR-03 Scope of the uncertainty intervals stated; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. The intervals cover the listed assumptions, not the model's own error.
- **Evidence:** [physics_nozzle.md](../../reports/physics_nozzle.md), [validation_synthetic.md](../../reports/validation_synthetic.md), [physiology_real.md](../../reports/physiology_real.md)
- **Needed:** 3D CFD comparison on coronary geometries (P6), a patient-specific flow, an input interval wide enough for real patients' flow, and agreement with invasive FFR.

### H-07: Surrogate error or extrapolation

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The learned surrogate differs from the reduced-order solver, most of all for inputs outside its synthetic training ranges.
- **Hazardous situation:** The surrogate's FFR crosses 0.80 where the solver's does not.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02, if the user relies on the surrogate's value.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P4 Probable = 16 unacceptable. Measured on synthetic test vessels: 0.24% disagree with the solver at 0.80 (agreement 99.76%). On real images extrapolation is common: 19 of 20 ARCADE gallery cases were outside the training ranges (mostly vessel length).
- **Controls:** SR-18 Surrogate parity and shared pre-processing; SR-19 Physically shaped surrogate output; SR-20 Surrogate accuracy against the solver; SR-21 The solver is the reference; SR-22 Model interval (split-conformal); SR-02 Plain-language warnings with every result
- **After controls:** P2 Remote = 8 reduce. Estimated: the solver's FFR is shown beside the surrogate's, plans are re-checked with the solver, and out-of-range inputs are warned; the user would have to prefer the surrogate's value against the reference shown next to it.
- **Evidence:** [surrogate_eval.md](../../reports/surrogate_eval.md), [qca_gallery.md](../../reports/qca_gallery.md)
- **Needed:** Training ranges that cover real angiograms; a usability check that users read the solver value.

### H-08: Wrong vessel or path analysed

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The user clicks a neighbouring vessel, or the path runs through a side branch, the catheter or an overlapping vessel.
- **Hazardous situation:** The profile, FFR and plan belong to another vessel or a mix of vessels.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02 for the intended vessel.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P4 Probable = 16 unacceptable. Estimated; user-dependent and not measured.
- **Controls:** SR-08 Points off the vessel refused; SR-13 Analysed path shown on the image; SR-10 Lumen edges from the image intensity; SR-02 Plain-language warnings with every result
- **After controls:** P4 Probable = 16 unacceptable. The overlay lets the user check the path; whether users do is not measured.
- **Needed:** Usability study (SR-42); automatic vessel labelling (coronary segments).

### H-09: Plan used as a sizing instruction

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The user takes the suggested stent diameter and length as the device to implant.
- **Hazardous situation:** A stent sized from 2D QCA (with H-01, H-03, H-05 errors) is chosen without intravascular imaging or the operator's own sizing.
- **Harm:** Oversizing: dissection, perforation. Undersizing: malapposition, thrombosis, restenosis.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P4 Probable = 16 unacceptable. Estimated; use-dependent and not measured.
- **Controls:** SR-43 Absolute lumen size; SR-26 Generic catalogue and sizing rule; SR-25 A virtual stent only widens; SR-27 Shortest plan reaching the target; SR-09 Pixel size and units; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P4 Probable = 16 unacceptable. The sizing rule and the oversize flag limit gross errors only. Measured on rendered vessels: intensity edges alone read the reference diameter about 13–15% narrow; the scaled lumen (SR-43, the default since P5b) removes this on renders, but its absolute accuracy on real angiograms is unknown, so a suggested diameter can be too small or too large.
- **Evidence:** [two_view_synthetic.md](../../reports/two_view_synthetic.md)
- **Needed:** Instructions that the plan is an estimate, sizing compared with IVUS or OCT, and a usability check.

### H-10: Post-PCI FFR overestimated

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The virtual stent restores an ideal lumen: no recoil, malapposition, side-branch jailing or diffuse disease outside the stent, and an unchanged microcirculation.
- **Hazardous situation:** A plan predicted to reach the target leaves residual ischaemia.
- **Harm:** Persistent angina, repeat revascularisation.
- **Before controls:** S3 Serious × P5 Frequent = 15 unacceptable. Estimated; post-PCI predictions are not compared with post-PCI wire FFR.
- **Controls:** SR-15 Microvascular resistance calibration; SR-24 Grey-zone flag; SR-03 Scope of the uncertainty intervals stated; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P5 Frequent = 15 unacceptable. Not measured.
- **Needed:** Post-PCI invasive FFR for validation.

### H-11: Wrong analysis frame

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The chosen frame of a run is before full opacification, during washout or blurred by motion.
- **Hazardous situation:** An under-filled vessel shows a false narrowing or hides a lesion.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Measured on CADICA test patients: with the P7 end-diastolic rule the analysis frame lies outside the span of the clinicians' selected frames in 28 of 177 runs (16%); with P4d's peak rule 32 (18%) with the D53 segmenter and 22 (12%) with M5 (D54, D55). On synthetic beating hearts it is within one frame of end-diastole in 95% of runs.
- **Controls:** SR-05 Cine runs on one intensity scale; SR-06 End-diastolic analysis frame in the opacified phase; SR-44 End-diastolic frames from the DICOM or the images; SR-13 Analysed path shown on the image
- **After controls:** P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Measured with the controls (the CADICA result is the product's own rule).
- **Evidence:** [cine_frames.md](../../reports/cine_frames.md), [cine_synthetic.md](../../reports/cine_synthetic.md)
- **Needed:** A lesion-aware frame choice; real runs with ECG to confirm the end-diastolic frames; the user's confirmation.

### H-12: Wrong measured flow velocity used

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The contrast front is timed wrongly (overlapping vessels, the catheter, a wrong heart cycle, a coarse frame rate), one view foreshortens the path, the injection itself adds flow, or a resting run is converted with a relation that does not fit the patient.
- **Hazardous situation:** The user chooses the measured velocity and the FFR is too high or too low.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P3 Occasional = 12 unacceptable. Measured on held-out synthetic beating-heart runs only: two views, median velocity error 5.8%, 98% of cases within ±25%, distal FFR changed by 0.005 (median; 90th percentile 0.022) by the velocity error alone; one view −16% (foreshortening). Not measured against real flow.
- **Controls:** SR-31 Measured flow velocity is shown, and used only when the user chooses it; SR-46 Measured flow velocity with guards, phase correction and interval; SR-45 Heart motion followed through the run; SR-44 End-diastolic frames from the DICOM or the images
- **After controls:** P3 Occasional = 12 unacceptable. The fixed velocity stays the default. The measured one is used only on request and only when every guard passed (13% of synthetic runs rejected); its calibrated interval then enters the input Monte Carlo. Its accuracy on real runs is unknown.
- **Evidence:** [cine_synthetic.md](../../reports/cine_synthetic.md), [cine_calibration.md](../../reports/cine_calibration.md)
- **Needed:** Agreement with real flow (a flow phantom, or Doppler-wire velocities) before the measured velocity could become a default.

### H-13: Result attributed to the wrong patient

- **Cause / sequence of events:** Files are exported, renamed and loaded by hand; the software holds no patient identity.
- **Hazardous situation:** A result is read for another patient's angiogram.
- **Harm:** A wrong decision for that patient (as H-01 or H-02).
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P3 Occasional = 12 unacceptable. Estimated; depends on the site's handling of files.
- **Controls:** SR-36 Traceable case report; SR-04 One image loader
- **After controls:** P3 Occasional = 12 unacceptable. The report records the input's SHA-256, which proves which file was analysed but not whose it is.
- **Needed:** DICOM / PACS integration with patient identity checks under the site's procedures.

### H-14: Unit or conversion error

- **Cause / sequence of events:** A length or pressure is converted wrongly between mm / m or mmHg / Pa.
- **Hazardous situation:** Diameters, pressures and FFR are wrong by a constant factor.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P2 Remote = 8 reduce. Estimated.
- **Controls:** SR-09 Pixel size and units; SR-14 Reduced-order FFR model; SR-17 Invalid geometry refused
- **After controls:** P1 Improbable = 4 acceptable. Estimated: SI everywhere, conversions in one tested module, physics sanity bands checked in clinical units.
- **Needed:** Keep the conversions in units.py; review new input and output boundaries.

### H-15: Patient data disclosed

- **Cause / sequence of events:** De-identified study data are committed to the repository, the app is exposed on a network, or a third-party component is compromised.
- **Hazardous situation:** Patient data reach people who should not have them.
- **Harm:** Breach of privacy (not a physical injury; handled with the security risk process).
- **Before controls:** S2 Minor × P2 Remote = 4 acceptable. Estimated.
- **Controls:** SR-33 Patient data never in the repository; SR-34 App local by default; SR-35 Verified downloads and safe extraction; SR-39 Declared and listed third-party software
- **After controls:** P2 Remote = 4 acceptable. Estimated: tracked locations are refused, the app is local by default, downloads are verified, the SBOM lists every component.
- **Needed:** A threat model and security activities under IEC 81001-5-1; vulnerability monitoring of the SBOM.

### H-16: Software failure during a procedure

- **Cause / sequence of events:** A crash, hang or slow update while the patient is on the table.
- **Hazardous situation:** The analysis is delayed or unavailable.
- **Harm:** Longer procedure, more contrast and radiation.
- **Before controls:** S2 Minor × P3 Occasional = 6 reduce. Estimated.
- **Controls:** SR-29 Interactive stent updates; SR-30 Planner speed; SR-32 Robust console output; SR-17 Invalid geometry refused
- **After controls:** P2 Remote = 4 acceptable. Estimated: interactive updates within 300 ms and the planner within 1 s are tested; invalid inputs are refused with a message.
- **Evidence:** [app_latency.md](../../reports/app_latency.md), [planner_benchmark.md](../../reports/planner_benchmark.md)
- **Needed:** Reliability testing on the target hardware; a fallback (the procedure continues without the software).

### H-17: Use outside the intended population

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The software is used for bypass grafts, left main or ostial lesions, in-stent restenosis, the culprit vessel of an acute myocardial infarction, severe microvascular disease or poor-quality images.
- **Hazardous situation:** An FFR is shown where the model's assumptions do not hold.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Estimated; nothing detects these situations.
- **Controls:** SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere; SR-02 Plain-language warnings with every result
- **After controls:** P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Labelling only; no automatic detection.
- **Needed:** Exclusions in the instructions for use, automatic detection where possible, clinical data per subgroup.

### H-18: Wrong or corrupted model file

- **Cause / sequence of events:** A model file is replaced, corrupted or paired with the wrong normalisation statistics.
- **Hazardous situation:** Segmentation or surrogate outputs are wrong.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P2 Remote = 8 reduce. Estimated.
- **Controls:** SR-07 Segmentation in ONNX Runtime with the training pre-processing; SR-18 Surrogate parity and shared pre-processing; SR-36 Traceable case report; SR-38 Strict configuration and provenance
- **After controls:** P1 Improbable = 4 acceptable. Estimated: mismatched statistics and non-segmentation models are refused; model hashes are recorded in the case report.
- **Needed:** Signed model files in a release.

### H-19: Display misread

- **Cause / sequence of events:** The user reads the curve instead of the distal value, misses the grey-zone flag, misunderstands what the intervals cover, or over-trusts a single number.
- **Hazardous situation:** The output is understood differently from what it says.
- **Harm:** As H-01 or H-02.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Estimated; no usability evaluation has been done.
- **Controls:** SR-24 Grey-zone flag; SR-03 Scope of the uncertainty intervals stated; SR-02 Plain-language warnings with every result; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P5 Frequent = 20 unacceptable. Not measured (SR-42 is a gap).
- **Needed:** Usability engineering under IEC 62366-1 with interventional cardiologists.

### H-20: Wrong 3D reconstruction not noticed

- **Cause / sequence of events:** In two views, the traced path follows a crossing vessel, the start and end points are not the same landmarks in both views, the heart is in a different cardiac phase in the two runs, or the projection angles are read with the wrong convention.
- **Hazardous situation:** The 3D length and the diameters are wrong, so the FFR and the stent length are wrong, while the result looks like a two-view measurement.
- **Harm:** As H-01, H-02 or H-05.
- **Before controls:** S4 Critical × P4 Probable = 16 unacceptable. Measured on synthetic cases: with views chosen without overlap, 5 of 100 were off by more than 5% in length (worst −21%, 5.9 mm 3D error, views 33° apart) and the check flagged none of them. With random views the length error reached 5.7% (90th percentile) where the vessel overlaps. Phase and convention errors are not measured.
- **Controls:** SR-41 Foreshortening-free lengths; SR-02 Plain-language warnings with every result; SR-01 Research-use notice everywhere
- **After controls:** P3 Occasional = 12 unacceptable. Smoother fitting (8 mm knots, D50) cut large length errors to 1 in 200 synthetic cases. The figure lets the user check the path in both views; the ray-gap and residual check flags uncorrected motion, but no check caught the large errors (a reliability warning was tried and dropped, D51). Not verified on real data.
- **Evidence:** [two_view_synthetic.md](../../reports/two_view_synthetic.md)
- **Needed:** Real two-view runs (or a phantom) to confirm the angle convention (D49) and to measure how often large errors occur on real data; end-diastolic frame matching (P7); overlap detection on the traced path.
