# Cine runs: end-diastolic frames and flow velocity on synthetic beating hearts (P7)

> **Research use only — not for clinical decisions.** Synthetic data, not clinical data: these runs show that the method works where the truth is known, not that it agrees with real flow.

Generated by `vstent cine-synthetic`. Do not edit by hand.

**Cases:** 60 synthetic cases (seeds 9200–9259, held out: no method or setting was chosen on them), two runs each from two C-arm views, 120 runs in all (`configs/cine_synthetic.yaml`, `vstent.imaging.synthetic_cine`). P5's coronary trees beat on a heart sphere: the heart is largest at end-diastole (the ECG R wave) and contracts by 8%–15% with a twist. Breathing adds a slow drift. Heart rate 50–110 bpm, 7.5, 15 or 30 frames per second. Contrast fills the tree at a resting velocity of 0.1–0.35 m/s (cycle average), 1.5–2.5 times faster in diastole than in systole, then washes out. Each run is a DICOM with the frame time and C-arm geometry; 50% of runs also carry an ECG and the R-wave pointer.

**Method (the product's steps):**
- every frame is segmented with the exported segmenter;
- ED frames: the R-wave pointer, else the ECG's R peaks, else the images (the vessel tree's spread, largest at end-diastole);
- the analysis frame: the ED frame in the opacified window with the most vessel;
- the centreline between the landmarks clicked on it;
- the contrast front's arrival along it, following the heart;
- the velocity: the slope of distance on flow time, which corrects for phasic flow with a diastolic / systolic ratio of 2 (D55). Distances come from one view or from the two-view 3D path.

Intervals: 90% split-conformal, the measured velocity × / ÷ 1.68 (one run) and × / ÷ 1.23 (the mean of two runs, used only when they agree within ×1.5), calibrated on development seeds 9164–9199 (`reports/cine_calibration.md`). One view: the upper end × 1.37 for foreshortening. FFR: the true lumen with the FAVOR Pilot hyperemic velocity of the measured vs the true resting velocity.

## Flow velocity

- Two views (3D path; 51 cases, 40 with an accepted velocity, 0 reconstructions failed): median |error| 5.8%, median error -1.2%; within ±25%: 98%; interval coverage 95%. 3D length error, median |error| 1.2%.
- FFR from the measured vs the true velocity (two views): median |difference| 0.005, 90th percentile 0.022.
- One view (107 runs, 104 accepted): median error -16.1% (foreshortening makes it negative), median |error| 16.6%; widened-interval coverage 95%.
- Runs rejected by the guards (or not analysable): 13%. Reasons seen: 0.01 m/s is outside 0.05–0.8 m/s; 1.04 m/s is outside 0.05–0.8 m/s; heart cycle unknown; imprecise; the contrast front does not advance steadily along the path.

**Limits:**
- The phasic correction assumes the same flow profile family that the generator draws from (systole ≈ 0.31 s, ratio 1.5–2.5), so its benefit here is partly by construction.
- Real coronary flow profiles vary more (vessel, microvascular disease, arrhythmia).
- The contrast injection itself adds flow.
- Agreement with real flow needs a phantom or a Doppler wire (HUMAN_ACTIONS).
