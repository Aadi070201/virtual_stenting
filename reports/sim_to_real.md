# Sim-to-real: segmentation trained with rendered angiograms (P8)

> **Research use only — not for clinical decisions.**

Generated by `vstent sim-to-real-report`. Do not edit by hand.

Baseline: the M5 segmenter, trained on ARCADE's 1,000 syntax training images. New: the same configuration (seed, schedule, early stopping on ARCADE val) with 1000 rendered angiograms (`vstent synth-angio`, `reports/synth_angio_card.md`) added. Dice per image at probability ≥ 0.5; paired difference = new − baseline per image, with a 95% percentile bootstrap interval (10,000 resamples).

**Labels differ by design.** ARCADE annotates the SYNTAX segments of each image, often not every major vessel in view; the rendered labels mark every vessel ≥ 1.5 mm (pre-registered). On ARCADE, a segmenter that finds unannotated vessels is scored down for it.

**Adoption rule** (pre-registered in docs/PLAN.md): the new model replaces the product's segmenter only if its mean ARCADE test Dice is higher and the lower bound of the interval is above −0.01. **Outcome: adopted.**

ARCADE val chose both models' epochs, so its numbers are optimistic; ARCADE test was evaluated once for this comparison. The rendered test split comes from the same generator as the new model's training images.
