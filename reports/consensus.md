# Multi-frame consensus on real angiograms (P4g)

> **Research use only — not for clinical decisions.** Repeatability, not accuracy: a consensus that repeats can still be wrong.

Generated by `vstent consensus-report`. Do not edit by hand.

**Question:** P4f found that one automatic frame does not measure a lesion stably (D57). Does the median over K frames of the same lesion repeat well enough?

**Data:** P4f's measurements of CADICA's expert lesions (CC BY 4.0; default segment margin): 5894 lesion boxes, 456 lesions with ≥ 1 measured keyframe. Patients split as in P4b: 21 development, 21 test.

One frame, from the same measurements (P4f's frame-to-frame result, all patients): %DS CV 44.6%, FFR CV 18.7% (414 lesions).

**Method (pre-registered in docs/PLAN.md, P4g, before any consensus was computed):** for each lesion with ≥ 2K measured keyframes, two disjoint sets of K frames (alternate keyframes in time order, the first K of each); the median %DS and the median segment FFR of each set; the CV between the two medians (root mean square over lesions, as in P4f). K is chosen on the development patients: the smallest K in 1–5 meeting both targets (FFR CV ≤ 9.4%, QFR's inter-observer CV in QREP; %DS CV ≤ 11.9%, QCA's), otherwise the lowest FFR CV. The test patients are scored once, with that K. Intervals: 95% patient-level bootstrap.

- 111 test lesions with ≥ 10 measured keyframes (20 patients). %DS CV 27.0% (95% 20.1%–32.5%); FFR CV 5.1% (95% 3.4%–6.8%); within-lesion FFR SD 0.040.
- The two medians on the same side of 0.80: 91.0%.

### Instability flag (test patients)

A consensus is flagged when its 5 frames' FFR values lie on both sides of 0.80.

- Consensus sets flagged: 31.5%.
- Lesions whose two medians fall on opposite sides of 0.80: 10; flagged in either set: 100.0%.
- Lesions whose medians agree: 101; flagged in neither set: 62.4%.

## Sensitivity: tracks with two boxes on one keyframe

Found after the result above, while preparing a demonstration: 16 lesion tracks (9 development, 7 test; 601 boxes) hold two boxes on the same keyframes: two boxes of one grade in one video were joined into one track. Their alternate keyframes can mix two lesions. Without them (not pre-registered; nothing above is changed by it):

- Development: K = 5 by the same rule.
- Test, K = 5: 106 lesions; FFR CV 4.9% (95% 3.1%–6.7%); %DS CV 24.6%; medians agree at 0.80 in 91.5%.

## Limits

- CADICA's keyframes span heart phases; the product takes its consensus over end-diastolic frames (P7), which differ less from one another.
- Frames share the segmentation model's habits, so their errors are partly correlated: the median removes frame-to-frame noise, not a bias that every frame has (D44: severe lesions read too mild).
- The FFR is the lesion segment's at the assumed 0.3 mm/px (CADICA has no pixel size), as in P4f. Nothing here is compared with wire FFR.
