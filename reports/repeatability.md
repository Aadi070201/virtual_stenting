# Repeatability on real angiograms (P4f)

> **Research use only — not for clinical decisions.** Repeatability, not accuracy: a measurement that varies when nothing about the patient changes cannot be accurate, but a repeatable one can still be wrong.

Generated by `vstent repeatability`. Do not edit by hand.

**Data:** CADICA (CC BY 4.0): 470 expert lesions graded 0–98% in 42 patients, 5894 lesion boxes on keyframes; all patients (a reporting use; nothing was chosen on these results).

**Measurement (the product's steps):** segmentation; the vessel path through the box, extended on every side by a margin; QCA with the product's lumen; %DS at the worst point inside the box; the segment's FFR from the reduced-order solver at the assumed 0.3 mm/px (CADICA has no pixel size). This FFR is the lesion segment's, not a whole vessel's. Coefficient of variation (CV): root mean square of SD / mean per lesion (or per frame), as in QREP. Intervals: 95% patient-level bootstrap.
