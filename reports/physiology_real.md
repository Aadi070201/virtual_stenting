# The model's physiology against real invasive measurements (P4e)

> **Research use only — not for clinical decisions.** Real patients' invasive measurements, without angiograms: these check the model's physiology assumptions, not its FFR from images.

Generated by `vstent physiology-real`. Do not edit by hand.

**Data:** DEFINE-FLOW (Johnson, Kirkeeide and Gould, JACC Cardiovasc Interv 2021, doi:10.1016/j.jcin.2021.07.041; data doi:10.5061/dryad.h18931zm6, CC0). 455 lesions of 379 subjects with a core-lab-accepted pre-PCI measurement and its raw tracing: Doppler instantaneous peak velocity, aortic and distal pressure, ECG, with the core lab's three-beat windows at rest and at peak flow. Values per lesion are the median over its measurements. The checks and their rules were pre-registered in docs/PLAN.md before the data were downloaded.

## Phasic coronary flow (P7's prior)

Each resting beat (ECG R wave to R wave) is fitted with P7's two-level profile: the systole duration that best splits the beat's velocity into a lower and a higher mean, and the ratio of the later (diastolic) to the earlier (systolic) mean. The ratio does not depend on the Doppler peak-to-mean factor.

| | Median | Interquartile range | 10th–90th percentile | Lesions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Diastolic / systolic ratio at rest | 1.73 | 1.50–2.01 | 1.26–2.39 | 455 |
| Systole duration at rest (s) | 0.39 | 0.35–0.42 | 0.30–0.45 | 455 |
| Diastolic / systolic ratio at hyperemia | 1.63 | 1.43–1.88 | 1.28–2.22 | 453 |

Resting ratio by artery: LAD 1.77 (1.57–2.03, n 291); LCx 1.81 (1.58–2.13, n 85); RCA 1.35 (1.17–1.63, n 71).

P7 assumes a ratio of 2 (its synthetic hearts draw 1.5–2.5) and a systole of 0.31 s. **Rule** (pre-registered): the prior is replaced if the median resting ratio lies outside 1.5–2.5. **Did not fire: the prior stays.**

## Hyperemic velocity (the fixed 0.35 m/s)

The stenosis-free hyperemic mean velocity per lesion is estimated as k × APV at peak flow ÷ FFR, k being the Doppler mean-to-peak factor (0.5 for a parabolic profile; about 0.8 measured in vitro). The model's fixed velocity is 0.35 m/s; P1 varies it over 0.25–0.45 m/s.

| k | Median (m/s) | Interquartile range | 10th–90th percentile | Share inside 0.25–0.45 m/s | 0.35 m/s inside the IQR |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0.5 | 0.21 | 0.16–0.26 | 0.13–0.34 | 27% | no |
| 0.8 | 0.33 | 0.25–0.42 | 0.21–0.55 | 56% | yes |

**Rule** (pre-registered): the fixed velocity is revisited if 0.35 m/s lies outside the interquartile range at both k. **Did not fire.**

## Pressures and the lesions

- Aortic pressure at hyperemia: median 92 mmHg (IQR 82–102); 78% inside P1's 70–110 mmHg.
- FFR (core lab): median 0.84 (IQR 0.76–0.89); CFR: median 2.17 (IQR 1.75–2.58).
- CONTRAST's tracings carry no markers of when contrast or adenosine was given, so the pre-registered rest / contrast / adenosine comparison could not be made reliably and is not reported.

**Limits:** Doppler measures a peak velocity at the wire tip, distal to the lesion, and the wire alters the flow it measures; DEFINE-FLOW's patients had stable disease and intermediate lesions; no angiograms, so nothing here measures FFR from images.
