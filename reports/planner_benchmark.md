## Settings

- Catalogue (generic): diameters [2.25, 2.5, 2.75, 3.0, 3.25, 3.5, 3.75, 4.0] mm, lengths [8.0, 12.0, 15.0, 18.0, 22.0, 26.0, 30.0, 34.0, 38.0] mm.
- Start positions every 0.5 mm; stent must cover a lesion MLD (DS > 30%); nominal diameter ≤ 1.1 × reference at the distal end; DS < 30% over the first and last 2 mm; inside the vessel; up to 2 stents (pairs from the best 20 candidates per lesion, spread over all lengths, no overlap).
- Stent edges blended over 1 mm inside each end; expansion 1. Pa, Pv and R_micro fixed at their pre-PCI values.

## Limitations

- Synthetic single vessels; post-PCI FFR comes from the same reduced-order model (or its surrogate), not from patients.
- The stent model only widens the lumen to the stent radius; no recoil, malapposition, edge injury or side-branch effects.
- Two-stent plans pair the best candidates per lesion, which is not an exhaustive search; overlapping stents are not considered.
